Before the Flood

Safety Tips

  • Prepare an emergency kit with water, food, and medications.
  • Know your flood risk and evacuation routes.
  • Secure your home by elevating appliances and valuables.
  • Stay informed via local weather alerts.

During the Flood

Emergency Contacts

  • National Flood Hotline: 1-800-FLOOD-HELP
  • Local Emergency: 911
  • Weather Service: 1-888-999-9999

Do's and Don'ts

  • Do: Move to higher ground immediately.
  • Don't: Drive through flooded roads.
  • Do: Listen to authorities.
  • Don't: Use electrical appliances in water.

After the Flood

Health Advice

Avoid contaminated water. Boil water before drinking. Watch for mold and use protective gear.

Sanitation Info

Clean surfaces with bleach solution. Dispose of damaged food and items safely.
